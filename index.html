The Fabric

420 GSM French terry, brushed on the inside.

Most hoodies sold here are cut for a European winter or an American mall. Kosha's terry is milled heavier where it matters — hood, pouch, cuffs — and brushed lighter across the body, so it breathes through a Mumbai commute and still holds heat on a Manali morning.

Fit & Sizing

Cut oversized. Fits true to the label.

Kosha is designed to sit loose through the body and sleeve. If you prefer a closer fit, we recommend sizing down once. All measurements are body measurements in centimetres, taken flat.

Between sizes? Choose the smaller size for a cropped, boxy fit, or the larger size for maximum slouch.
